# HLT-02 — Patient Registration & Patient Identity (Master Patient Index - MPI)
## Specification & Functional Scope

- HLT-02-001: Register new patients with unique enterprise MRN (Medical Record Number) across facilities.
- HLT-02-002: Store patient demographics: Full legal name, date of birth, sex, blood type, primary phone, email, address.
- HLT-02-003: Store national ID (14 digits Egyptian NID), passport number, and insurance policy details.
- HLT-02-004: Patient profile photograph, document attachments, and scanned identification.
- HLT-02-005: Emergency contacts and next-of-kin relationship information.
- HLT-02-006: Guardian registration for minors and authorized legal representatives.
- HLT-02-007: Deep search by name, phone, MRN, National ID, or barcode.
- HLT-02-008: AI & Fuzzy matching for duplicate patient record detection.
- HLT-02-009: Multi-branch patient record merge with full historical audit trail and rollback guard.
- HLT-02-010: Patient communication preferences (SMS, WhatsApp, Email, Call).
- HLT-02-011: Multi-branch shared patient access with clinic-level consent verification.
- HLT-02-012: Comprehensive demographic change history and correction audit trail.
